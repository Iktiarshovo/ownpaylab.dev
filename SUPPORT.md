# Getting Support for OwnPay & OwnPay Lab

Thank you for building with OwnPay. Whether you are integrating our checkout SDKs, building autonomous agent payment workflows, or evaluating our settlement specifications, multiple support channels are available.

---

## 1. Official Documentation & Guides

Before reaching out, check our comprehensive public documentation:

- **Developer Platform & Live Documentation:** [https://www.ownpaylab.dev](https://www.ownpaylab.dev)
- **Architecture & Specifications:** [Architecture Overview](./docs/architecture.md) & [Protocol Specifications](./specifications/README.md)
- **Integration Guides:** [Developer Quickstarts](./developers/quickstarts/node-quickstart.md)
- **Frequently Asked Questions:** [FAQ](./docs/faq.md)

---

## 2. Community & Public Discussion

For general development questions, integration tips, and architectural discussions:

- **GitHub Discussions:** [Join OwnPay Discussions](https://github.com/Iktiarshovo/ownpaylab.dev/discussions)
- **GitHub Issues:** Use GitHub Issues **only** for reporting reproducible software bugs or specific feature requests in our open-source tools.
  - [Open a Bug Report](https://github.com/Iktiarshovo/ownpaylab.dev/issues/new?template=bug_report.md)
  - [Open a Feature Request](https://github.com/Iktiarshovo/ownpaylab.dev/issues/new?template=feature_request.md)

---

## 3. Merchant & Integration Support

If you are a merchant currently onboarded or undergoing testing on the Merchant Platform:

- **Merchant Portal:** [https://ownpaylab.tech](https://ownpaylab.tech)
- **Integration Support Desk:** `support@ownpaylab.tech`
- **Hours of Operation:** Sunday – Thursday, 09:00 – 18:00 UTC (Extended SLAs for enterprise partners)

---

## 4. Security & Vulnerability Reports

If you believe you have found a security vulnerability in any OwnPay system, contract, or SDK:

- **DO NOT post publicly.**
- Email [**`security@ownpaylab.tech`**](mailto:security@ownpaylab.tech) directly.
- Full details and response SLAs can be found in our [Security Policy](./SECURITY.md).
