## 2. Vesting Schedules & Long-Term Lockups

To guarantee permanent alignment and eliminate short-term speculative behavior:

- **Founders & Core Engineering (60% Total):**
  - **4-Year Vesting Schedule** with a mandatory **12-Month Cliff**.
  - Monthly linear vesting thereafter.
  - Performance-linked milestone accelerations based strictly on audited Gross Settled Volume and platform security records.

- **Institutional Investors (25% Total):**
  - Subject to multi-year lockup agreements to protect the protocol from rapid secondary liquidation.
  - Reserved exclusively for value-add partners with demonstrated expertise in fintech, cryptography, and institutional market expansion.

- **Ecosystem Reserve (15% Total):**
  - Disbursed transparently via the OwnPay Grants Committee.
  - Governed by public milestone deliverables documented here in OwnPay Lab.
