## The Master Allocation Summary

OwnPay enforces a strict, transparent capital and equity allocation framework:

- **Founder & Co-Founder (Combined):** **`35%`** (Long-term commitment & milestone vesting)
- **Developer, Engineering & Infrastructure Ops:** **`25%`** (Core engineering contributors)
- **VC & Institutional Investors:** **`25%`** (Patient growth capital & institutional alignment)
- **Strategic Ecosystem & Community:** **`15%`** (Developer grants, liquidity, and bounties)
- **Total:** **`100%`**
