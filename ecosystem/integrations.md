# Supported Integrations & Protocols

OwnPay interfaces with premier blockchain networks, token standards, and developer platforms:

---

## Blockchains & Layer-2 Networks

- **Base:** Coinbase's Ethereum Layer-2 network (sub-cent gas fees, native USDC).
- **Arbitrum One:** High-performance EVM rollup for high-frequency trading and commerce.
- **Polygon PoS:** Low-cost, global payment infrastructure.
- **Solana:** Ultra-fast, high-throughput network for instant consumer and micropayment settlements.
- **Ethereum Mainnet:** Premier institutional settlement layer for large-value transfers.

---

## Wallet Standards

- **EIP-6963:** Multi-injected provider discovery.
- **WalletConnect v2:** Cross-platform mobile wallet bridge.
- **Solana Wallet Standard:** Universal Solana browser extensions and mobile wallets.
- **ERC-4337:** Account Abstraction smart accounts with gas sponsorship.
