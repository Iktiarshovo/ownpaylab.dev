# Partner Framework & Institutional Collaboration

OwnPay partners with forward-thinking enterprises, protocols, and liquidity providers who share our vision of open, durable, and non-custodial financial rails.

---

## Partnership Pillars

1. **Merchant Platforms & E-Commerce Enablers:** Integrations into e-commerce CMS platforms, payment orchestrators, and point-of-sale hardware manufacturers.
2. **AI & Machine Agent Platforms:** AI model providers, agent hosting infrastructure, and autonomous tool networks adopting MCP and x402 standards.
3. **Liquidity & Stablecoin Issuers:** Direct integrations with verified stablecoin issuers ensuring deep liquidity, atomic mint-and-burn mechanics, and minimal slippage.
